With Soli moving into beta it needs music! If you're an artist interested in joining then do [get in touch](mailto:frederik.obrien@gmail.com).

The signup steps are pretty straightforward. Your email address will be added to the beta list and connected to a newly created artist account. You'll be able to log in with the same email at https://dashboard.soli.network to manage your profile details, upload music, and view stats. Your music is kept in private [IPFS](https://docs.ipfs.tech/concepts/what-is-ipfs/) storage. The files are only accessible through Soli.

For payouts you'll be asked to set up a Stripe account, so we're able to send you weekly stream payouts. You'll start receiving these straight away.

If at any point you decide Soli's not for you, just say the word. We'll close your profile and delete your music. All good.
