Soli exists to support musicians - not exploit them. Here is what we promise:

* **You own your music**
* **You get paid directly and transparently**. There are no black boxes or mysterious payment equations. Every stream sends pennies your way
* **Your music is on the platform to be listened to, and nothing else.** We won’t sell your data, and your music won’t be used for AI ‘training’
* **We’re transparent**, with open code and open books, so you’ll always understand how payouts work
* **No ads, ever**

This project is about showing the status quo is a choice, not an inevitability.

Get involved. Maybe we can make the world a bit less shit.
